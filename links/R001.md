# R001 · Summer and Winter Sun Paths

**What is the path of the sun in summer and in winter?**

## Current recommended website

### [NOAA Solar Calculator](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/)

Provider: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory.

## Purpose

Find the sun's position, sunrise, sunset, and solar noon for a selected location, date, and time. Use the results to understand seasonal changes in solar elevation and azimuth and to inform site shading and overhang design.

## Usage notes

Select a location and set the date, local time, and time zone. Compare summer and winter solar positions to consider how shading elements can admit low-angle winter sunlight while blocking high-angle summer sunlight. Check the time zone and daylight saving time settings.
