# C03-02 · Typical Uncomfortable Winter and Summer Weather

**What is the weather like on a typical thermally uncomfortable day in winter and in summer?**

## Current recommended website

### [PVGIS Typical Meteorological Year tool](https://re.jrc.ec.europa.eu/pvg_tools/en/#TMY)

Provider: European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC), Photovoltaic Geographical Information System (PVGIS).

## Purpose

Obtain a Typical Meteorological Year (TMY) dataset for a selected location. The course uses hourly weather data to examine how outdoor thermal conditions vary through a typical day and during uncomfortable winter and summer conditions, and to support later thermal-comfort modeling.

## Course context

Thermal-comfort models may require inputs such as air temperature, humidity, solar radiation, terrestrial radiation, and wind. Before using a downloaded file, check its fields, units, timestamps, time zone, and radiation definitions against the requirements of the specific model. Do not assume that one TMY download contains every variable needed by every thermal-comfort model.

## Usage notes

1. Search for a location or enter its latitude and longitude.
2. Open the **TMY** section and select the available period or dataset options.
3. Download the result in the required format. PVGIS currently offers CSV, JSON, and EPW options; CSV can be opened directly in Excel.
4. Review the metadata and column definitions before analysis.
5. Select representative uncomfortable winter and summer conditions according to the method specified in the course; a TMY dataset itself is a constructed typical year and is not automatically a record of the most extreme historical event.

If PVGIS becomes unavailable or unsuitable, edit **Current recommended website** on this page to point to a replacement capable of producing comparable hourly meteorological data. Update the provider, available variables, formats, and usage notes while preserving this resource ID and path.
